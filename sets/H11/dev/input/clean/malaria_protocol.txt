MALARIA & DENGUE MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL (District Programme)

1. C/O fever for 2-7 days with chills: test with RDT before treatment.
2. Platelet count below 20000 /uL needs referral to Ward 5 (Medicine).
3. Paracetamol 650 mg TDS. Avoid NSAIDs.
4. ASHA workers do house-to-house fever surveillance every Monday.
5. Rule out Addison's disease and Wilson's disease if electrolytes are deranged.
